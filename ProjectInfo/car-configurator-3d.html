🚗 Car Configurator 3D

Platform: WebGL / Unity
Date: January 2026
Status: Live

Project Overview

Car Configurator 3D is an interactive Unity WebGL application that lets users browse multiple car brands and models, customize them in real time, and explore every detail from both inside and outside the vehicle. Vehicle content is loaded dynamically using Unity's Addressable Asset System, keeping the initial build small and allowing new cars, colors, and parts to be added without shipping a full update.

The project demonstrates a full showroom-style configurator workflow — from selecting a brand and model, to customizing paint, wheels, and interior, to interacting with doors, lights, and camera perspectives — all running smoothly in the browser.

Key Features

  • Multiple Brands & Models: Browse and select from a growing catalog of car brands and models
  • Addressables-Based Loading: Cars, materials, and parts are downloaded on demand via Addressables, reducing initial load size
  • Paint Color Customization: Switch between a range of colors and finishes with real-time material updates
  • Wheel Swapping: Choose from multiple wheel designs per vehicle
  • Interior Customization: Change interior trim and upholstery options
  • Interactive Doors: Open and close individual doors to inspect the cabin
  • Light Controls: Toggle headlights, taillights, and indicators on/off
  • Dual Camera Modes: Switch between an exterior orbit camera and an interior first-person view
  • Smooth Orbit Controls: Rotate, zoom, and pan around the vehicle for a full 360° inspection

How It Works

1. Select a Brand & Model: Choose from the catalog — the corresponding car asset bundle is fetched on demand through Addressables.

2. Customize Your Car: Adjust paint color, wheel style, and interior trim using the configurator panel, with instant visual updates.

3. Interact With the Vehicle: Open/close doors, toggle headlights and taillights to inspect the car in different states.

4. Switch Camera Views: Move between an exterior orbit camera for full walk-around inspection and an interior view to see the cabin from the driver's seat.

5. Explore Freely: Rotate, zoom, and pan at any time to review your final configuration from any angle.

Development Highlights

  • Addressables Pipeline: Car models, materials, and wheel/interior variants are organized into addressable groups, allowing new vehicles to be added without rebuilding the whole app
  • Modular Customization System: Color, wheel, and interior swaps are handled through a reusable material/prefab-swapping architecture, easily extendable to new cars
  • Camera System: Smooth transitions between orbit and interior cameras using Cinemachine virtual cameras
  • Interactive Rigging: Door and light states are driven by animation/state controllers tied to UI buttons
  • Optimization: LOD and texture streaming considerations to keep WebGL builds performant across devices
  • Cross-browser Compatibility: Tested on Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari
